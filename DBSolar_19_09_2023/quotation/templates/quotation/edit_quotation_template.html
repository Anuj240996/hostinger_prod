ABOUT US

HERAMB INDUSTRIES and DB RENEWABLE ENERGY PVT. LTD. are incorporated in 2005 providers of Solar PV System Commissioning, development, construction, operation, and maintenance of PV Solar Power Systems. We have a strong professional design and engineering team comprised of highly qualified professionals in their field, many of whom are highly qualified engineers, installers, and team leaders, all trained and certified. Our team offers exceptional solar PV design and installation experience for homeowners as well as owners of commercial buildings, solar park for open access requirements of large industries. Our company is committed to promoting energy independence through the installation of solar systems in local communities. We take exceptional pride in all our work, from preliminary design through permitting, installation, final inspection, and interconnection with the utility company. All these procedures are looked after in-house, and we never hire sub-contractors for the same. We have continuous product research on the industry’s top-quality system components and are familiar with the use of those components. Because of our passion for this new and exciting renewable energy technology, we are constantly updating our skills. We train our staff continuously to stay updated with the latest National Electrical Code (NEC), local code requirements, and installation techniques. Our team has the ability to recommend a variety of solutions to meet your specific needs. Our experienced and certified electricians and team leaders work hard to give every homeowner a reliable solar system to generate clean green electricity safely. As a homeowner, you deserve the best, which is why we employ and train only the best in the industry to ensure you get a good return on your investment. We install the solar electric system with a five-year warranty on installations (labor and materials), a twenty-five-year manufacturer’s product warranty on the solar panels, and a five-to-ten-year warranty on inverters and solar system output. To date, we have completed residential, commercial, and industrial and solar park projects with capacities up to 150MW.
